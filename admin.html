Arrival/departure times per stop, from photo and video timestamps — plain data, no flags or warnings. You decide what's worth a closer look.
▸

Each gap between stops is compared against what it should realistically take (exit cushion at the last place + real driving time between those exact coordinates, via OpenStreetMap's routing — not an estimate + entry cushion at the next place, plus extra if that order needs office access) — the difference is "unexplained," not "wrong." A building with an elevator and one with stairs shouldn't be judged the same way.
