GSMS Privacy Policy

GS Solutions Inc. · Last updated September 25, 2026

This policy explains what information GSMS, the internal operations system of GS Solutions Inc., uses and how it is protected.

Information we use

  • Clients: business name, contact person, email, phone and property addresses, and the orders, photos and documents related to their service.
  • Staff and technicians: name, contact details, role and work assignments.
  • QuickBooks Online: with GS Solutions' authorization, GSMS reads GS Solutions' own customers, products and services, classes, departments, sales settings and recent estimates and invoices, and creates customers, estimates and invoices for completed work.

How we use it

Only to run GS Solutions' services: scheduling and completing work orders, keeping clients informed, and billing through QuickBooks.

Where it is stored

Data is stored in GS Solutions' Microsoft 365 (SharePoint) and processed by our hosting provider, Vercel, in the United States. QuickBooks connection tokens are stored in the same Microsoft 365 environment and are never shown in the browser.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent any information. We do not share it with third parties except the service providers that run GSMS (Microsoft, Vercel and Intuit) and when required by law.

Security and retention

Access requires an authorized account, and all traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Information is kept while it is needed for GS Solutions' business and accounting records.

Your choices

Clients can ask us to update or delete their information using the contact details below. The QuickBooks connection can be removed at any time from QuickBooks.
